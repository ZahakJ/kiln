# Kiln brand guide

**Where work gets made.**
*An office suite with a little fire in it.*

Kiln is a free, open-source (AGPL-3.0) office suite for Linux, Windows, macOS and Android. It replaces Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote with Kiln Write, Grid, Deck and Notes, and Kiln Home ties them together. The brand has one idea: **a kiln is where soft things become things that last.** Every app is a glazed tile shaped like a kiln arch. The suite mark is the kiln itself, with a live flame in its mouth.

Office users should feel at home in the first ten seconds: File tab, ribbon, status bar, the same shortcuts. After that, Kiln should feel warm, crafted and its own, never like a recoloured Office or a generic SaaS product.

## 1. The mark

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo.svg`, `logo-512.png`, `logo-1024.png` | Full-colour kiln: tenmoku dome, glowing mouth, flicked flame. The default mark. |
| `logo-mono.svg`, `logo-mono-bone.svg`, `logo-mono-currentColor.svg` | Single colour. The mouth is cut out and the flame stands in it, with a knocked-out gap between the body and the back tongue so it reads as fire, not a droplet. Use it for print, embossing, themed icons and anywhere you only have one ink. |
| `wordmark.svg` (bone, for dark backgrounds), `wordmark-light.svg` (ink, for light backgrounds) | Lowercase **kiln** drawn from Fraunces Black. The dot on the i is the flame. |
| `lockup.svg` (dark backgrounds), `lockup-light.svg` (light backgrounds), `lockup-mono.svg`, `lockup-mono-bone.svg` | Mark and wordmark together. This is the primary signature. PNGs are in `png/`. |
| `construction.png` | Geometry of the arch, the mouth, the slab and the glyph safe area. |

**Construction.** The tile is a superelliptic kiln arch, `|x/L|^2.6 + |y/H|^1.6 = 1`. The 1.6 crown pulls it toward a catenary, the curve real kilns are built on, so it reads as an arch and not a gumdrop or a tombstone. The 2.6 walls meet the hearth almost vertically. On a 512 grid the tile is 440 wide and 412 tall, with r 40 hearth corners and a 20 px slab underneath. The mouth is the same arch at 0.54 W × 0.64 H, standing on a 20 px sill. The flame is a round belly whose tip flicks right, with an ember-red back tongue leaning left and a white-hot core (the body at 0.42 scale). All of this is generated by `src/geo.py` and `src/icons.py`, so every tile is optically identical.

**Clear space.** Leave at least the height of the **n** on every side of the lockup, and 25 % of the tile width around the mark on its own.

**Minimum sizes.**
- Lockup: 24 px tall on screen, 12 mm in print. Below that, use the mark on its own.
- Colour mark: 24 px. Below 24 px, use the flat tile (`icons/svg/suite-32.svg`). At 16 px, use the pixel-fitted tile (`icons/svg/suite-16.svg`). Never scale the hero artwork down.
- Wordmark on its own: 16 px x-height.

**Wordmark rules.** It is artwork. Never retype "kiln" in Fraunces to fake it. Never set it in capitals, outline it, or swap the flame for a dot. In running text the product is written **Kiln** (capital K), and the apps are **Kiln Write**, **Kiln Grid**, **Kiln Deck**, **Kiln Notes** and **Kiln Home**.

## 2. App icons

Each app burns its own colour. Ember belongs to the suite.

| App | Replaces | Glaze | Glyph |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kiln Home | the Office start screen | tenmoku (brown-black, rust break) | the kiln: mouth and flame |
| Kiln Write | Word | cobalt | a heavy geometric pilcrow ¶ |
| Kiln Grid | Excel | jade | a headed table with a selected cell and its fill handle |
| Kiln Deck | PowerPoint | ember | a pie chart with one slice pulled out |
| Kiln Notes | OneNote | saffron | a tilted page with a folded corner |

No glyph is a letter, no glyph is a 2×2 window, and none of them repeats the flame. The pilcrow, the fill handle and the pulled slice are things Office users click every day, so they map instantly.

**One tile, four levels of detail.** Use the version drawn for the size you need:

| Size | File | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 128 px and up (dock, store, hero) | `icons/svg/<app>.svg` | Glazed tile: pooled glaze at the foot, a soft sheen on the crown, glaze-break highlight on the rim, slab and contact shadow. |
| 40–96 px | `icons/svg/<app>-48.svg` | The same, without the hero rim highlight. |
| 20–40 px | `icons/svg/<app>-32.svg` | Flat glaze, a 1 px lighter crown rim, a heavier glyph. No gloss and no grain. |
| 16 px | `icons/svg/<app>-16.svg` | Drawn pixel by pixel on the integer grid (`src/px16.py`). Notes becomes a page with a solid fold and two ruled lines. The suite becomes a dome, a rim and a flame. |
| Monochrome | `icons/mono/<app>.svg`, `<app>-currentColor.svg` | Glyph only, one colour. For Android themed icons, favicons, website navigation and in-app toolbars. |

**Ready-made exports**
- `icons/png/<app>-{16,24,32,48,64,128,256,512,1024}.png`. Each size uses its own level of detail.
- `icons/macos/<app>-1024.png` (+ `.svg`): macOS canvas, with the tile on an 824 px body, 100 px margins and a soft shadow.
- `icons/windows/<app>-256.png`: full-bleed tile for `.ico` and the Start menu.
- `android/<app>-{background,foreground,monochrome}.{svg,png}`: 432 × 432 adaptive-icon layers (108 dp at xxxhdpi). Content stays inside the 66 dp safe circle. The product foregrounds keep the kiln arch as a tonal shape behind the glyph, so the family survives circle and squircle masks.
- `favicon.ico` (16 pixel-fitted, 32 flat, 48 full) and `favicon.svg`.
- `icons/size-check.png`: every size on bone, white, cobalt and soot.

## 3. Colour

All values live in `tokens.json` and `tokens.css`. Fill colours are for shapes. Text colours are tuned separately and every text pair passes WCAG 2.1 AA (the ratios are listed in `tokens.json` → `contrast`).

| Glaze | App | Fill | Solid button / File tab | Text on light | Text on dark | Tint light / dark |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cobalt | Write | `#3159C6` | `#2D4FB5` + white | `#2D4FB5` | `#97AEF4` | `#E7ECFA` / `#1C2440` |
| Jade | Grid | `#1D8A60` | `#17714E` + white | `#17714E` | `#6FD0A6` | `#E2F2EA` / `#142C22` |
| Ember | Deck | `#E05A27` | `#E05A27` + **near-black** `#1C0D06` | `#B2431A` | `#F7976B` | `#FBE8DE` / `#36190E` |
| Saffron | Notes | `#E3A126` | `#E8A82C` + **near-black** `#1C0D06` | `#87590A` | `#F2C35C` | `#FAF0D9` / `#30230C` |
| Tenmoku | Home | `#2E1C13` | ember `#C2481C` + bone | `#B2431A` | `#F7976B` | `#F6E6DA` / `#2A1A12` |

The black-on-ember and black-on-saffron buttons are deliberate. They give poster contrast and they pass AA.

**Neutrals.** Bone `#FFF6E8`, bisque `#EADBC6`, terracotta `#B4462A`, clay `#7A4A33`, tenmoku `#2A1A12`, soot `#17110E`.
**Fire.** Core `#FFF3D6`, flame `#FFB547`, ember `#FF6A26`, tongue `#D93D12`. The fire colours are used only for flames, sparks, glows and progress, never for large backgrounds.

**UI surfaces.** In light mode: background `#F6EFE6`, panel `#FFFCF7`, page `#FFFFFF`, text `#2B1C14`. In dark mode: background `#17110E`, panel `#201813`, page `#221A15`, text `#F5EBDD`. Dark mode is tuned, not inverted. The document page sits on a soft ember glow (`--kiln-shadow-page`).

Using the tokens: link `tokens.css`, set `data-theme="light|dark"` (or let `prefers-color-scheme` decide) and `data-app="write|grid|deck|notes|home"` on `<html>`. Then build with `--app`, `--app-solid`, `--app-on-solid`, `--app-text` and `--app-tint`, so one component library serves every app.

## 4. Type

| Role | Face | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | Fraunces, outlined | wght 900, SOFT 40, WONK 0, opsz 144 |
| Display, headlines, app names, card titles | Fraunces | 700–800, SOFT 40, WONK 0, optical size auto, tracking −1 to −2 % at large sizes |
| Interface | Figtree | 400 body, 500 tabs, 600 buttons, 700 labels, 800 eyebrows (uppercase, +12 % tracking) |
| New documents | Figtree 11 pt body, Fraunces headings | This is the "Kiln" document theme. The body is neutral so Word users never open a blank page in a surprise font. |
| Office compatibility | Carlito / Caladea | These are substituted for Calibri / Cambria so imported documents keep their line breaks. |

Both families use the SIL OFL 1.1 licence. See `fonts/LICENSES.md`. Numbers in tables and status bars use tabular figures. No monospace appears in the brand or the chrome.

## 5. Signatures in the product

These small, repeatable moments make Kiln feel like Kiln without getting in the way:

- **The arch.** The active ribbon tab is an arch-topped door that opens into the ribbon. The active sheet tab in Grid is the same arch, upside down. Home cards and phone cards are kiln-arch doors in the full app colour.
- **The File button.** A solid block in the app's colour with a small spark. It is the one place each app shouts its colour.
- **The spark.** A four-point spark with shortened side arms (so it never reads as a plus). It marks the File button, the tip of the text caret and the "Take the tour" button, and it floats as loose embers in banners. Use one at a time.
- **The flame chip.** "Saved to Studio" and "Autosave on" carry the flicked flame. Saving is the only state that gets a flame.
- **Warm light.** The document page glows faintly ember in dark mode. Home has a warm light falloff from the top left. Progress bars (word goal, storage) run ember to flame.
- **Selection.** Text selection is a soft ember wash `--kiln-selection`. The focus ring follows the app colour.

**Familiar on purpose.** Keep these exactly where Office users expect them: the title bar with quick save/undo/redo, File + Home/Insert/Layout/References/Review/View, labelled ribbon groups, the Styles gallery, the formula bar, slide thumbnails, notebook > section > page, the status bar with page, words, language, proofing, view modes and zoom, and every standard shortcut.

## 6. Voice

Kiln talks like a good studio-mate: plain, warm, and a little playful. Say what happens.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| The kiln is warm. What are we making today? | Welcome to your productivity hub! |
| Saved to Studio | Your changes have been successfully synchronised |
| Your .docx, .xlsx and .pptx open as they are. | Seamless interoperability with legacy formats |
| Fresh from the kiln | Recommended templates for you |
| 216 words to go | You are 86 % of the way to your goal! 🎉 |

Taglines:
- Primary: **Where work gets made.**
- Supporting: **An office suite with a little fire in it.**
- Secondary, for the licence and community: **Fired in the open.**

Never: "empower", "unleash", "supercharge", "seamless", exclamation marks in the UI, or emoji in product copy.

---

## 7. Do and don't

**Do**
- Use the glazed tile at 128 px and up, the flat tile from 20 to 40 px, and the pixel tile at 16 px.
- Put the lockup on bone, white, soot or tenmoku, or the mono lockup on a solid glaze.
- Let each app use its own colour for its File tab, primary button, active tab text and focus.
- Use ember for suite-level actions: Home's New button, the onboarding tour, brand moments.

**Don't**
- Put a letter (W, X, P, N) in a tile, or put the glyphs in rounded squares.
- Add gradients to UI chrome. The only gradients are glaze on the tiles, fire, and the warm light falloff.
- Recolour the tiles, swap glyphs between apps, or put the flame on product tiles.
- Rotate, stretch, outline or drop-shadow the lockup, or place the colour mark on a busy photo.
- Use cobalt for Home or ember for Write. Each colour belongs to one app.
- Use the 512 artwork at 16–32 px. It has its own drawings.

```
  wordmark.svg  wordmark-light.svg  lockup.svg  lockup-light.svg  lockup-mono*.svg   png/ (lockups + wordmarks, 1200 px)

  icons/png/      <app>-16 … 1024.png
  icons/mono/     single-colour glyphs
  icons/macos/    <app>-1024.png/.svg      icons/windows/  <app>-256.png
  android/        <app>-background|foreground|monochrome.svg/.png (432 × 432)
  favicon.ico  favicon.svg
  tokens.json  tokens.css
  fonts/          Fraunces-VF.ttf  Figtree-VF.ttf  OFL texts  LICENSES.md
  banner.svg/.png  banner-light.svg/.png  (2000 × 600)   social-preview.png/.svg (1280 × 640)
  mockups/        write|grid|deck|notes|home-{light,dark}.png (1440 × 900), phone-{light,dark}.png (390 × 844 @2x)
  mockups/src/    the HTML/CSS behind every mockup (uses tokens.css; open in a browser)
  construction.png  board.png  icons/size-check.png
  src/            generators: geo.py, icons.py, px16.py, wordmark.py, build_*.py, mock_*.py, render.mjs
```

Rebuild everything with `src/` (Python 3 + fonttools + uharfbuzz + Pillow, and Node + Playwright for rendering). Every asset is generated from the same geometry, so a change to `geo.py` or `icons.py` flows through to every size.
