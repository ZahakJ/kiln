The Autumn Firing

Twice a year we load the big catenary kiln together. This is the plan for October: what goes in, how hot we take it, and who stays up for the overnight shift.

What goes in the kiln

We have room for roughly 240 pieces across six shelves. Stoneware takes the back of the chamber, where the flame path is longest. Porcelain and the test tiles sit up front so we can watch them through the spy hole. Please wax your feet before Friday, and label anything glazed with the new tenmoku so we can track how it breaks over the rims.

  • Bisque by Wednesday, glaze by Thursday noon
  • Cones 9, 10 and 11 on every shelf
