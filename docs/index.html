Free and open source, no account

The office suite for your Word, Excel and PowerPoint files.

Write documents in Kiln Write, work out the numbers in Kiln Grid and present with Kiln Deck. Kiln opens your .docx, .xlsx and .pptx as they are and saves them back the same way. It runs in your browser with nothing to install, and your files stay on your device.

Your files

Your .docx, .xlsx and .pptx open as they are

  • .docx
  • .xlsx
  • .pptx
  • .csv
  • .md
  • PDF
  • No import, no conversion. Word, Excel and PowerPoint files open directly and save back in their own format.
  • What Kiln does not edit, it leaves alone. Embedded objects, SmartArt and macros stay in the file through a save. Macros never run.
  • Line breaks where they were. Kiln brings fonts with the same measurements as Calibri, Cambria, Arial, Times New Roman and Courier New.
  • Check before you send. The Compatibility Checker lists anything the format cannot hold, and Inspect Document finds comments and hidden text.
  • PDF from every app, and OpenDocument files in the Linux desktop app.

Three apps

Documents, spreadsheets and presentations

Kiln Write, Kiln Grid and Kiln Deck all open from Kiln Home, with your recent files and the templates.

Kiln Write

Documents, in place of Word

Reports, letters and newsletters, laid out the same on screen, on paper and in PDF.

  • Styles, sections, columns, headers and footers, footnotes and tables of contents
  • Track Changes and threaded comments for reviewing with others
  • Version history: name a version, compare it with now, restore it
  • Focus mode, word-count goals and Markdown shortcuts as you type
  • Print with a preview, or export a PDF

Kiln Write in the manual

Kiln Grid

Spreadsheets, in place of Excel

Excel formulas work as written, including dynamic arrays and LAMBDA.

  • 505 functions, with their arguments shown as you type
  • Charts, PivotTables, conditional formatting, sort and filter
  • Formula Lab: watch a formula work out its answer step by step
  • Big sheets: a million-cell workbook opens in the browser in 2.76 s

Kiln Grid in the manual

Kiln Deck

Presentations, in place of PowerPoint

Your .pptx with its masters, layouts and themes, and a slide show with Presenter View.

  • Presenter View: your notes, the next slide and a timer, while the audience sees the slide
  • Transitions, animations and the Animation Pane
  • Design Ideas suggests layouts for the slide you are on
  • Charts and tables on slides
  • Handouts and notes pages, printed or as PDF

Coming from Office

You already know where everything is

The ribbon, the File menu, the status bar and the shortcuts are where Office users look for them, in a light or a dark theme.

  • The ribbon with the tabs each Office app has, classic or simplified. Ctrl+F1 collapses it.
  • The File menu: recent files, templates, Info, Save As, Print with preview, Export and Options.
  • The status bar, with page and word counts in Kiln Write, sums and averages of the selection in Kiln Grid, and zoom.
  • Office's shortcuts and Key Tips on Alt. Every shortcut is in the manual.
  • Office's words: search for “Merge & Center” or “Format Painter” and the command palette finds it.

Wherever you work

On your desk, on your phone, offline

Kiln fits the screen it is on. On a phone it has a layout of its own: a toolbar at the bottom for what you have selected, and the whole ribbon one tap away. With a keyboard, the shortcuts work as on the desktop.

Kiln on a phone or tablet

Private

Kiln lays out and saves your documents on your device, in the browser as on the desktop. There is no account and no telemetry.

Offline and installable

After the first visit Kiln starts, opens and saves without a connection. Install it from Chrome or Edge for a window of its own and Open with ▸ Kiln in your file manager.

Edit together

Share a Kiln Write document or a Kiln Deck presentation and edit it together, live, with everyone's cursor in their colour. Changes pass through a small relay you run yourself, and edits made offline merge when you reconnect. How to share

Version history

Kiln Write documents and Kiln Deck presentations keep their versions on your device. Name one, preview it, restore it.

Arabic, right to left

The interface in English, German or Arabic. In Arabic it is mirrored, as Office is, and documents and sheets run from the right.

Templates

36 finished documents, workbooks and decks with real content to start from. Grid templates calculate: change a figure and the totals and charts follow.

How it is built

  • Everything that computes, from layout and formulas to the file formats and PDF, is one Rust core.
  • In the browser it runs as WebAssembly; on Linux it runs natively in a Tauri window.
  • kiln-cli converts, renders and inspects documents from a script.
