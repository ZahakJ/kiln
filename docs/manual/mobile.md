# Kiln on a phone or tablet

Kiln fits the window it is in. Below 640 pixels wide (a phone, or a narrow browser window) it
has a phone layout; from 640 to 1023 pixels (a tablet) it keeps the desktop's window with the
Simplified ribbon; from 1024 pixels on, the desktop layout. The commands are the same
everywhere: the phone layout shows the ribbon in another form, and a hardware keyboard has the
desktop's [keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md).

## Kiln Home

On a phone, Kiln Home shows the doors two by two (Document, Workbook, Deck and Templates), your
recent, pinned and shared files under them, and a tab bar at the bottom: **Home**, **Files**,
**Shared** and **Templates**. The **New** button stands above the tab bar and lists the three
blank documents; the page fades out under it, and scrolls up clear of it at its end.

## The top bar

A document on a phone has one bar at the top:

- **Back** (the arrow) closes the document, asking about unsaved changes as Close does, and goes
  back to Kiln Home.
- **The document's name**, with its extension. Tap it to rename the document.
- **Undo** and **Redo**.
- **Search** finds any command by its name, as Search does on the desktop (Alt+Q).
- **More options** (⋮): **Share**, **Save**, **Save As**, **Print**, **Version History**,
  **Find and Replace**, the app's view (in Kiln Write, **Print layout**), **File** (the File
  menu: New, Open, Info, Export and the rest) and **Settings** (File ▸ Options).

## The bottom toolbar and the ribbon

The bar at the bottom holds the commands you use most for what is selected:

- **Text** (Kiln Write, and text in Kiln Deck and in a cell being edited): bold, italic,
  underline, font colour, highlight, bullets, numbering, alignment and indent.
- **A cell** (Kiln Grid): number format, bold, italic, fill colour, borders, merge, wrap text and
  alignment.
- **A slide** (Kiln Deck, nothing selected): new slide, layout, text box, picture, shapes, table
  and icons. **A shape**: fill, outline, Quick Styles, Arrange, copy and paste.

Swipe the bar sideways for the rest. A command with a menu (bullets, borders, font colour) has
its arrow beside it.

The first button names a ribbon tab, **Home** at first, and opens the **ribbon**: a sheet over
the lower part of the screen with the tabs of the desktop ribbon as a row of chips (Home,
Insert, Layout, References, Review, View, and a contextual tab such as Table Design while its
object is selected) and each group of the tab as a tile under its desktop name (Clipboard, Font,
Paragraph, Styles). The ↘ in a tile's corner opens the group's dialog, as on the desktop. Drag
the sheet down, tap outside it, or press Back to close it; the bottom toolbar then names the
tab you were on.

## Back

Back closes what is open first: the ribbon, Share, a menu, a dialog or the File menu. With
nothing open, it closes the document (asking about unsaved changes) and shows Kiln Home. The
browser's Back button does the same in Kiln on the web.

## Share

**More options ▸ Share** sends a copy of the document:

- **Send a copy** sends the file as it was last saved, through the phone's share sheet (mail,
  messaging, cloud storage). Save first: a document with unsaved changes, or one never saved,
  says so.
- **Send as PDF** sends a PDF of the document as it is now.
- **Share for live editing** shares the document for [collaboration](collaboration.md).

In a browser that cannot share files, the first two are **Download a copy** and **Download as
PDF**.

## The on-screen keyboard

The bottom toolbar rides on top of the on-screen keyboard, so bold, italic and the ribbon stay
in reach while you type, and the text you type stays above it.

## Kiln Write on a phone

Documents open in **Web Layout**: the text flows to the width of the screen, with no page edges,
so there is no sideways scrolling. **More options ▸ Print layout** shows the pages as they print;
choose it again to go back. View ▸ Views in the ribbon has the same choices.

## Touch

Everything a mouse does in a document, a finger does too:

- **Tap** places the caret, selects a cell or selects a shape.
- **Hold** (a long press) selects the word or the cell under your finger and opens the same menu
  as a right-click there.
- **Drag a handle** to change a selection: the teardrops at the ends of selected text, the round
  handles at the corners of a range of cells. A selected picture or shape has 28-pixel handles to
  size it and a rotation handle above it to turn it.
- Over a selection made with a finger, a **toolbar** floats with **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**,
  **Comment**, **Format** (the ribbon, on its Home tab) and **More** (the right-click menu). Tap
  the active cell again in Kiln Grid to show it there.
- **Pinch** to zoom the page, the sheet or the slide. **Double-tap** fits the width to the
  screen (the page in Print layout, the columns in use in Kiln Grid, the slide in Kiln Deck);
  double-tap again for a closer look, and once more for the width. In Kiln Deck a double tap on a
  shape edits its text.

A mouse and the keyboard work as on the desktop at the same time.

## Kiln Grid on a phone

A finger scrolls the sheet; a tap selects a cell, a column or a row. A tap on a cell does not
bring up the on-screen keyboard: tap the **formula bar** to type in the cell (or to change what it
holds). With the keyboard up, the formula bar sits on top of it with **✕** (cancel) and **✓**
(enter), and a row of quick keys under it: **123** for the number keys, **fx** to insert a
function, **A→Z** for the letters, and = ( ) : , $ % for formulas.

The sheets are a row of chips above the bottom toolbar: tap one to show it, **+** to add one, and
hold one for its menu (Rename, Delete, Tab Color and the rest). With a range selected, a chip
beside them shows its sum (or count); tap it for the average, count, minimum and maximum.

A filter button's menu and a cell's list (Data ▸ Data Validation, Alt+Down) open as sheets from
the bottom of the screen.

## Kiln Deck on a phone

A presentation opens on its slides, one under another. Tap a slide to edit it across the width
of the screen, and **+** to add a slide after the current one; hold a slide for its menu
(Duplicate, Delete, Hide Slide and the rest). Back goes from the slide to the list.

Under a slide being edited: the previous and next slide, where you are, **Notes** to show or hide
the speaker notes under the slide, and **▶** to present from it (**Present**, over the list,
starts from the first slide).

## Presenting on a phone

**Present** fills the screen and turns to landscape (a browser that cannot turn the screen asks
you to turn the phone). Swipe to the next or the previous slide, or tap: the right of the slide
goes on, the left third goes back. **Notes** shows the slide's speaker notes over its foot. Back
or the show's **End show** button ends the show.
